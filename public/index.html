Which account’s follows should we dig through?

Strata reads the public posting history of everyone you follow and picks out media posts you have never liked or reposted — then shows them at random, from any point in time.

Everything stays in this browser. There is no account, no password and no server — the index is built from public AT Protocol records and stored locally in IndexedDB. Nothing about what you have liked ever leaves your machine. Already have an export?

Sign in to Bluesky

Liking, reposting and bookmarking need your permission. Strata sends you to Bluesky to approve it — your password is never typed into this page, and the access it gets can be revoked from your Bluesky settings at any time. Reading and downloading work without signing in.
